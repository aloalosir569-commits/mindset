This Privacy Policy explains how Mindset Reset Press (“we,” “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you visit https://mindset-ebook.pages.dev, order an e-book, contact us, or opt in to receive SMS and WhatsApp messages.

Our commitment: Customer phone numbers and opt-in data are never sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in information is not shared with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact and order details: your name, email address and order information when you request or purchase the e-book.
  • Phone number and messaging consent: your mobile number, the date and method of your opt-in, and your message history with us, only if you choose to receive messages.
  • Support communications: the content of emails, calls, forms or messages you send us. Our website forms open your own email app, so the information reaches us when you send that email.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by a payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on this website.
  • Basic technical data: standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, used for security and reliability.

2. How we use information

  • To process your order and deliver your e-book.
  • To send order confirmations, e-book delivery updates and support replies by email, SMS or WhatsApp where you have opted in.
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • To keep records of consent, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

3. How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not share phone numbers or opt-in data with third parties for marketing. We share information only with service providers that help us operate, and only as needed to perform their services. These include payment providers, email providers, and messaging providers that carry our SMS and WhatsApp messages (such as Twilio and WhatsApp). These providers may not use your information for their own marketing. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

4. SMS and WhatsApp messaging

If you opt in, we use your mobile number only to send the order, delivery and support messages described on our SMS & WhatsApp Notifications page. Message frequency varies, and msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and it does not run third-party analytics.

6. Data retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, to resolve disputes and to meet legal, tax and record-keeping requirements. Consent records are kept as proof of your opt-in. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, and you may opt out of messages at any time by replying STOP. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know, delete and correct personal information and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law. To make a request, email morganflex1t@hotmail.com.

8. Children’s privacy

Our website and e-book are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last revised.
