Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our e-books, ordering and delivery, messaging and privacy.

The e-books

What do you publish?

Short, practical e-books on mindset, focus, calm and resets, and habits. Our library currently has 12 titles. Browse the library.

Which e-book should I start with?

If you want one book that covers all four topics, start with The Calm Execution Playbook. Otherwise, pick the topic that matches your biggest challenge right now.

Is this medical or therapy advice?

No. Our e-books offer general educational information only and do not replace care from a licensed professional. See our Terms of Service.

Can I read a sample first?

Yes. Request a sample chapter and we will reply by email.

Ordering & delivery

How do I order?

Use the order page. Choose your e-book, send your request and we will email you the next steps and your download link.

Can I order more than one e-book?

Yes. Choose “Multiple titles” on the order page and list the e-books you would like in the message box.

What format are the e-books?

They are digital e-books delivered through a download link sent to your email.

I did not receive my download link. What should I do?

Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it is not there, email support@mindset-ebook.pages.dev or call +1 (302) 230-6641 and we will help. See our Delivery & Refund Policy.

Text & WhatsApp messages

Do I have to give my phone number?

No. A phone number is optional, and consent to receive messages is not a condition of purchase.

What messages will I receive?

Only order confirmations, e-book delivery updates and replies to your support requests. We do not send promotional or marketing texts through this program.

How often will you message me?

Message frequency varies with your orders and support requests, usually about one to four messages per order or inquiry. Msg & data rates may apply.

How do I stop messages or get help?

Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Details are on our SMS & WhatsApp Notifications page.

Privacy

Do you sell my information?

No. Customer phone numbers and opt-in data are never sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing. Read the Privacy Policy.

How can I ask you to delete my data?

Email morganflex1t@hotmail.com and tell us what you would like removed. We will respond to your request.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we will get back to you.
