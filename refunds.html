This policy explains how our e-books are delivered and how we handle refund requests.

1. Digital delivery

Our e-books are digital products. We deliver each one by sending a download link to the email address you provide. No physical item is shipped.

If you opted in to messaging, we may also send you a text or WhatsApp update when your link is sent. See SMS & WhatsApp Notifications.

2. If you did not receive your link

Check your spam or promotions folder first. If you still cannot find it, email support@mindset-ebook.pages.dev or call +1 (302) 230-6641 with the email address used for your order, and we will send a new link.

3. Refunds

Because the e-book is a digital product, we review each refund request individually. If you have a problem with your order or cannot access the file, contact us as soon as possible and we will work with you in good faith. We will reply to your request by email.

4. Your license

Your purchase includes a personal, non-transferable license to read the e-book. See our Terms of Service for details.
