SMS & WhatsApp Notifications

Optional text and WhatsApp updates about your order, your e-book delivery and your support requests.

How you opt in

You are only enrolled if you choose to be. You can opt in in these ways:

  1. On this website. Enter your mobile number and check the unchecked-by-default consent box on the Order form, the Contact form or the form below.
  2. During a support inquiry. When you email or call us, ask to receive replies by text or WhatsApp and confirm your mobile number and consent.

Providing a phone number is optional. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

What you will receive

  • Order confirmations after you place an order.
  • E-book delivery updates, such as when your download link is sent or when you need a new one.
  • Support replies to questions you send us.

These messages are transactional and service-related. We do not send promotional or marketing texts through this program.

Consent language shown on our forms

I agree to receive SMS and WhatsApp messages from Mindset Reset Press about my order, e-book delivery and support requests at the mobile number I provide. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Program details

  • Program name: Mindset Reset Press SMS & WhatsApp Notifications
  • Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on your orders and support requests. Most customers receive about one to four messages per order or inquiry.
  • Msg & data rates may apply. Standard rates from your mobile carrier may apply to messages you send and receive.
  • Need help? Reply HELP to any message, email morganflex1t@hotmail.com or call +1 (302) 230-6641.
  • Opt out anytime: Reply STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages unless you opt in again.
  • WhatsApp: We message you on WhatsApp only if you opted in. You can reply STOP at any time or block the business in WhatsApp.
  • Your data: Phone numbers and opt-in data are never sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt in to notifications

Add your details, check the box and send. Your email app will open with your opt-in ready to send.

Pressing the button opens your email app with your message prefilled. Nothing is sent until you press Send there. The messaging box is optional and unchecked by default.
